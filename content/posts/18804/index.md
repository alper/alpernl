Two notes on this piece about two track AI users:

I agree that if the only LLM you can use is Copilot, you may as well not use one at all. You're better off not getting exposed to what is really the MS Teams of AI.

It is a fantasy that an Enterprise can rally a team and plan, design and deploy their own vetted AI offering on any relevant timescale. Things develop from one week to another (not compatible with 6-12 month project timelines) and the expertise to follow developments is extremely rare.
