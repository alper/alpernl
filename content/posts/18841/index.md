> **No individual in the organization will ever have an accurate mental model about how the entire system works**. To put it simply: It’s the stuff we don’t know that bites us. There’s always stuff we don’t know.

> **if the people in the organization know more about the system, they are going to make better decisions in the future**

This kind of thinking can be a tough sell indeed and the instant gratification approach that is being sold along with AI is hardly conducive to look at more long term outcomes and more holistic approaches.
