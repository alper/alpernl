It's not so clear cut as it's made out to be here. Of course writing code should never have been your bottleneck to begin with. What LLMs have done is to remove “I need to write the code” as an excuse.

That has put significant shear on different parts of the system which is a Good Thing. Now we're tasked with having to figure out how to make those parts better. Before this development, companies could hide their lack of ideas and bad processes behind “it takes the engineers 6 months to ship a feature.” Now they can't anymore.
