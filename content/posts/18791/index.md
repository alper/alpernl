The CDU wants to abolish the right to work part-time in Germany. Most people don't know that anybody here can ask to work part-time and if the company does not have a good reason to refuse it, they can't.

There are a number of issues with this idea:

1. For as long as I've worked in Germany I don't think I've ever seen another man work part-time. In effect this would be abolishing a right that is only exercised by women which makes this sexist, even by the CDU's standards.
2. Given the discouraged labor participation of women in Germany in general and them being the only ones using this, it's fair to say that working part-time is rare in Germany. That makes the change here relatively inconsequential.
3. That majority of people who did work full-time up until now, have hardly created the economic output and innovation to put Germany in a competitive position. That makes you think part-time is not the real issue at hand here.

Like we're used to, another bit of ‘window dressing’ politics from the CDU without real reason or consequence.

(Full disclosure I work part-time in my current job and have worked part-time in the two jobs before that as well.)
