Shoehorning the open source forge into the same web application where corporate software engineering takes place was a historic mistake. It would be good to cut these two very different things loose from each other and explore what makes sense for each use case.

Open source is facing lots of challenges around trust, effort and financing fueled in large part by the LLM explosion in code. At the same time corporate software engineering is becoming faster and more industrial (also thanks to LLMs) with massive requirements in logic, gating and repository sizes.
